## How to put on the armor — overarching principle

*(SRC-0003, SRC-0004)*

The armor is not put on in one dramatic moment. The Seminary Teacher Manual (SRC-0004) cites President M. Russell Ballard comparing spiritual armor to chain mail — protection built from "numerous smaller acts woven together." ⚠️ *Paraphrase from SRC-0004 — original talk not independently verified.* Daily habits are the links. No single act constitutes complete armor; all six pieces worn together form the protection Paul describes.

Elder Neal A. Maxwell, as cited in the Seminary Teacher Manual (SRC-0004): "Putting off the natural man makes possible putting on the whole armor of God, which would not fully fit before." ⚠️ *Exact wording from SRC-0004 — original talk not independently verified.*

## Belt of truth — loins

**What it protects (SRC-0003, SRC-0004):** Moral purity, chastity, virtue
**The vulnerability (SRC-0004, citing President Harold B. Lee):** The loins are among "the most vulnerable to the powers of darkness." ⚠️ *Exact wording from SRC-0004 — original talk not independently verified.*

**How to put it on:**
- Live honestly — in speech, actions, and private thought
- Honor the law of chastity in conduct and media consumption
- Seek truth actively: scripture study, prayer, following the prophet
- The belt holds everything else in place — truth is the foundation piece that makes the other armor function

**Key observation:** In Roman warfare, the cingulum (belt) was the first piece donned and the foundational anchor for all other equipment. Truth is the spiritual equivalent — without it, nothing else holds.

## Breastplate of righteousness — heart

**What it protects (SRC-0003, SRC-0004):** Affections, emotions, loyalty, conduct
**The vulnerability:** The heart — what you love, what motivates you, where your loyalty truly lies

**How to put it on:**
- Repent regularly — the breastplate is kept clean through the Atonement
- Keep covenants, especially those made at baptism and in the temple
- Serve others — righteous conduct is daily, not theoretical
- Temple garment connection (SRC-0010, Elder Carlos E. Asay, Ensign August 1997): the garment is described as "a token of what Paul regarded as taking upon one the whole armor of God" — endowed members wear a physical reminder of the breastplate covenant

**As retrieved from SRC-0010** ⚠️ *retrieved via WebFetch — treat as verbatim pending direct confirmation against canonical URL*: "It is written that 'the white garment symbolizes purity and helps assure modesty...a token of what Paul regarded as taking upon one the whole armor of God (Eph. 6:13).'"

**Restoration note (DEC-0004, PASS-0007):** The D&C 27:16 footnote cross-references JS—H 1:35 — the physical breastplate Moroni delivered to Joseph Smith. The Restoration includes a literal counterpart to this piece.

## Shoes of the gospel of peace — feet

**What it protects (SRC-0003, SRC-0004):** Life course, goals, direction, objectives
**The vulnerability:** Where you are heading — letting the world set your life's direction rather than the gospel

**How to put it on:**
- Align your goals with the gospel — ask regularly whether where your life is going reflects gospel priorities
- Share the gospel — the preparation of the gospel of peace includes being ready to share your faith
- Walk with consistency — the Roman caligae (hobnailed boots) gave grip and endurance; spiritual shoes mean stability in the long walk, not just a sprint
- D&C Restoration connection (PASS-0007): "which I have sent mine angels to commit unto you" — the restored gospel brought by angelic messengers is what these shoes carry

## Shield of faith — whole body

**What it protects (SRC-0003, SRC-0004):** The whole soul — moveable, comprehensive protection
**The named threat (PASS-0001):** "The fiery darts of the wicked" — attacks that cloud spiritual vision

**How to put it on:**
- Daily scripture study — hold fast to the iron rod (1 Ne. 15:24, PASS-0008): "whoso would hearken unto the word of God, and would hold fast unto it, they would never perish; neither could the temptations and the fiery darts of the adversary overpower them unto blindness"
- Daily prayer — the shield requires constant contact with the Lord to remain effective
- Do not doubt — the stripling warrior principle (PASS-0010): "if they did not doubt, God would deliver them" — the mechanism of the shield is the quality of the faith behind it
- Build faith through experience — faith grows by acting on it, not just knowing about it

**Key teaching:** The shield is the only piece given a specific named threat to counter and is the only piece described as protecting the *whole* body. Paul names it "above all" — positionally held above and over everything else, covering all the other armor.

## Helmet of salvation — head

**What it protects (SRC-0003, SRC-0004):** Thoughts, intellect, mind
**The vulnerability:** What occupies your mind — the enemy attacks through what you see, hear, and think about

**How to put it on:**
- Fill your mind with light — choose media, music, and content that build rather than erode
- Maintain a settled assurance of your standing before God through the Atonement — the helmet is *salvation*, not anxiety about salvation
- *Perikephalaia* (G4030) — "encirclement of the head" — it wraps entirely around; there is no gap in protection from above
- Guard against the spiritual blindness named in 1 Ne. 15:24 — the fiery darts specifically target sight; a firm mind is the defense

**Note (SRC-0004):** President Harold B. Lee identified the head (thoughts, intellect) as among "the most vulnerable to the powers of darkness." ⚠️ *Exact wording from SRC-0004 — original talk not independently verified.*

## Sword of the Spirit — word of God

**What it is:** The only offensive piece; the only piece identified as belonging to the Spirit
**How it works:** *Rhema* (G4487) — not abstract scripture knowledge held generally, but the specific, active, Spirit-directed word spoken into a particular moment of temptation or need

**D&C addition (DEC-0004, PASS-0007):** "The sword of my Spirit, which I will pour out upon you, and my word which I reveal unto you" — two elements: the Spirit's power AND ongoing revealed word; the Lord promises to pour it out as a covenant, not just make it available

**How to put it on (use it):**
- Memorize scripture so specific passages are available in specific moments — not for display, but for deployment
- Seek personal revelation through prayer and the Holy Ghost — the *rhema* dimension means the sword is sharpened by the Spirit's living direction
- Jesus modeled this in the temptations in the wilderness (Matthew 4:4, 7, 10 / Luke 4:4, 8, 12 — SRC-0001) — each of Satan's three attacks was met with a specific, exact scripture applied to the specific attack ("It is written...")
- The sword is not swung in the abstract; it is used in a particular moment against a particular attack

## Prayer — the animating practice

*(DEC-0001 — prayer is a separate "how the armor works" section, not a seventh piece)*

Ephesians 6:18: "Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints."

Prayer is how the armor is put on and kept on. It is not a seventh piece; it is the ongoing act by which each piece stays in place and functions. D&C 27:18 adds "be agreed as touching all things whatsoever ye ask of me" — unity in prayer as a communal dimension of the armor.

## What General Conference teaches — summary of verified sources

*(SRC-0004 Seminary Teacher Manual references — attributions confirmed; exact wording not independently verified from original talks)*

**The critical principle:** Paul says "whole armour" twice. Partial protection is not the model. Every piece must be worn.

**GA teachings on record in the Seminary Manual (SRC-0004):**
- President Harold B. Lee — identified the loins, heart, feet, and head as "the most vulnerable to the powers of darkness"
- President M. Russell Ballard — chain mail analogy: true spiritual protection comes from "numerous smaller acts woven together," not dramatic single events
- Elder Neal A. Maxwell — putting off the natural man is prerequisite; the armor won't fully fit until the natural man is being overcome
- Elder Carlos E. Asay — temple garment is "another piece of armor worthy of our consideration" (verified verbatim from SRC-0010)
- Elder Robert D. Hales (SRC-0004) ⚠️ — modern temptations are more accessible than ever; the armor is urgently needed ⚠️ *paraphrase from SRC-0004 — original talk not independently verified*

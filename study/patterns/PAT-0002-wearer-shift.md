# Pattern: The Wearer Shift

- Pattern ID: PAT-0002
- Pattern name: The Wearer Shift — God and Christ wear the armor first; believers receive it
- Pattern type: Thematic / covenantal
- Strength of evidence: Supported
- Approval status: Draft — pending Chase review

---

## Passages included

- Isaiah 11:5 (PASS-0003) — Christ/the Branch wears righteousness and faithfulness as the girdle
- Isaiah 59:17 (PASS-0002) — God wears the breastplate of righteousness and helmet of salvation
- Romans 13:12, 14 (PASS-0005) — "put on the armour of light" immediately followed by "put on the Lord Jesus Christ"
- Ephesians 6:14–17 (PASS-0001) — believers are commanded to put on the same armor

---

## Exact shared features

The same armor pieces appear across multiple passages, but the *wearer* changes:

| Passage | Who wears the armor |
|---|---|
| Isaiah 11:5 | Christ (the Branch from Jesse) |
| Isaiah 59:17 | God himself |
| Ephesians 6:14–17 | Believers |
| Romans 13:14 | "Put on the Lord Jesus Christ" — putting on armor = putting on Christ |

The two pieces that appear most consistently across all wearers:
- **Breastplate of righteousness:** Isaiah 59:17 (God) → Ephesians 6:14 (believers)
- **Helmet of salvation:** Isaiah 59:17 (God) → 1 Thessalonians 5:8 / Ephesians 6:17 (believers)

---

## What this pattern may mean

*(Interpretation — labeled as such)*

The armor Paul describes in Ephesians 6 is not new equipment invented for believers. It is the same armor that God himself wore when he came to redeem Israel — the armor of Isaiah 59. Isaiah 59:16 states explicitly that God acted because "there was no man" and "no intercessor." God clothed himself in his own righteousness and salvation to do what no human could.

Paul then instructs believers to put on that same armor. The movement is from God → Christ → believers. Romans 13:14 makes the equation explicit: putting on the armor = putting on the Lord Jesus Christ. The armor is not a set of behaviors to perform; it is participation in the character of Christ himself.

This reading also explains why the spiritual qualities assigned to each piece are attributes of God and Christ (truth, righteousness, peace, salvation) rather than human virtues cultivated through effort alone.

---

## Disconfirming or complicating passages

- The pattern requires connecting Isaiah 59 to Ephesians 6 through Paul's literary dependence, which is supported by the verbal overlap but not explicitly stated by Paul — caution in presenting this as certain
- The garments of vengeance and cloak of zeal from Isaiah 59:17 are not carried into Ephesians 6 — Paul deliberately strips the retributive elements, which could indicate he is not simply reproducing Isaiah's armor but reshaping it for a different purpose

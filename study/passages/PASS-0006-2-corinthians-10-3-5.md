## Full KJV text (verses 3–5, verified 2026-10-26)

> 3 For though we walk in the flesh, we do not war after the flesh:
>
> 4 (For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds;)
>
> 5 Casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ;

---

## Textual observations

- No individual armor pieces are named — the "weapons" are unspecified but described as "mighty through God"
- The language is notably more *offensive* than Ephesians 6: "pulling down strong holds," "casting down imaginations," "bringing into captivity every thought"
- The enemy here is not named as the devil or principalities — the strongholds are intellectual and imaginative ("imaginations," "every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God")
- The goal is bringing thoughts into "the obedience of Christ" — a cognitive and volitional target, not a positional one (standing firm)

---

## Contrast with Ephesians 6

This passage uses warfare imagery more aggressively than Ephesians 6:

| Feature | 2 Corinthians 10:3–5 | Ephesians 6:10–18 |
|---|---|---|
| Direction | Offensive — pull down, cast down, bring captive | Defensive — stand, withstand, hold ground |
| Enemy | Intellectual strongholds, imaginations | Principalities, powers, rulers of darkness |
| Pieces named | None | Six specific pieces |
| Target | Obedience of Christ (cognitive) | Endurance in the evil day (positional) |

These are complementary pictures, not contradictory ones — but the emphasis and purpose differ significantly.

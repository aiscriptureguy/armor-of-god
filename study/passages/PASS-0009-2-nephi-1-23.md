## Full text (verified 2026-10-26)

> 23 Awake, my sons; put on the armor of righteousness. Shake off the chains with which ye are bound, and come forth out of obscurity, and arise from the dust.

---

## Textual observations

- "Put on the armor of righteousness" — a direct armor command, but in a single phrase rather than a piece-by-piece list
- The immediate contrast: put on armor / shake off chains — the armor is juxtaposed with bondage
- "Come forth out of obscurity" — the armor is associated with light and visibility, not hiding
- "Arise from the dust" — resurrection and renewal language; spiritual re-awakening
- The speaker is a dying father pleading with sons who have persistently rejected the gospel — the armor here is an urgent appeal, not a doctrinal teaching
- No individual armor pieces are named; the entire Ephesians 6 metaphor is compressed into a single phrase

---

## What this passage adds to the armor study

- **Armor vs. chains** — the most distinctive Book of Mormon contribution in this verse. In Ephesians 6, the armor is about standing against an external enemy. In 2 Nephi 1:23, not having the armor means being bound in chains — the enemy has already gotten inside. The armor is also about liberation from internal spiritual bondage, not just external defense.
- **One phrase for the whole** — "armor of righteousness" names the entire kit with a single quality. Righteousness is what the breastplate protects in Ephesians; here righteousness *is* the armor as a whole.
- **Urgency** — "Awake" opens the verse. Lehi's command is not calm instruction; it is a desperate father's call to sons who may not respond.

---

## Connections to other passages

- Ephesians 6:14 (PASS-0001): "breastplate of righteousness" — the Book of Mormon condenses the entire armor to the quality of the breastplate
- Isaiah 52:1–2: "Awake, awake; put on thy strength, O Zion... Shake thyself from the dust; arise" — Lehi is likely echoing Isaiah here
- Alma 5:7: "the chains of hell" — chains as spiritual bondage is a recurring BOM theme

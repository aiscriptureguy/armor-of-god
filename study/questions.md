# Study Questions — Armor of God

Format: `- [ ] <question text>`
Mark studied and approved questions with: `- [x] [studied - approved] <question text>`

---

## High Priority

- [x] [studied - approved] What are all the pieces of armor named in Ephesians 6:10–18, and what is the exact KJV wording for each?
- [x] [studied - approved] What body part does each piece of armor protect, and what is the spiritual parallel to that body part?
- [x] [studied - approved] Why is the sword (word of God) the only offensive piece? What does that asymmetry tell us about the nature of spiritual warfare?
- [x] [studied - approved] What do the Greek words behind each armor piece reveal about Paul's intended meaning?
- [x] [studied - approved] What did Roman military armor actually look like in Paul's time, and how closely does Paul's list map to actual equipment?
- [x] [studied - approved] What other passages in the Bible (Isaiah 59:17, 1 Thessalonians 5:8, etc.) use armor imagery, and how do they relate to Ephesians 6?
- [x] [studied - approved] Where does the armor of God appear in the Book of Mormon, and what distinct meaning does that context add?
- [x] [studied - approved] Where does the armor of God appear in the D&C (notably D&C 27:15–18), and how does the Restoration context change or deepen it?
- [x] [studied - approved] What do General Conference talks teach about putting on the whole armor of God in modern life?
- [x] [studied - approved] How does a person practically "put on" each piece of armor today? What does that look like daily?

## Medium Priority

- [ ] Are all pieces of armor equally emphasized in scripture, or do some receive more attention across the standard works?
- [ ] Is there a deliberate ordering to how Paul lists the pieces — head to foot, or most to least important?
- [x] [studied - approved] What is the role of prayer in the armor passage (Ephesians 6:18) — is it a separate piece or the means of putting on the armor?
- [ ] Are there passages in the standard works that describe removing or failing to put on the armor? What happens then?
- [x] [studied - approved] What is the identity of the enemy in Ephesians 6:12 ("principalities," "powers," "rulers of darkness")? How do Church sources interpret this?
- [ ] Does the Isaiah 59 armor passage (which Paul likely drew from) add Hebrew insights not visible in the Greek/NT text?
- [x] [studied - approved] What institute or seminary manual teaching on the armor of God is most useful and well-sourced?
- [ ] Are there any General Handbook references to the armor of God that inform Church application?

## Low Priority / Stretch

- [ ] Are there other world religious traditions with similar "spiritual armor" metaphors, and are any of them mentioned in approved Church sources?
- [ ] How has visual art historically depicted the armor of God — any patterns worth knowing for design reference?
- [x] [studied - approved] Are there any JST (Joseph Smith Translation) changes to the Ephesians 6 passage?
- [ ] Does the armor metaphor appear in the Doctrine and Covenants outside of D&C 27?
- [ ] Are there any Book of Mormon passages beyond the commonly cited ones that use armor or warfare metaphors in a spiritually instructive way?

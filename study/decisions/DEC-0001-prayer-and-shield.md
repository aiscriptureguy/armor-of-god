## Decision 1: Prayer (Ephesians 6:18) treatment on the site

**Question:** Should prayer be presented as a seventh armor piece on the interactive diagram, or as a separate section?

**Decision:** Option B — prayer is a separate "How the armor works" section below the diagram. The interactive warrior figure shows only the six named armor pieces. Prayer appears in a distinct section that explains it as the animating practice behind the armor, not a wearable piece.

**Rationale:** The text itself does not label prayer as a piece of armor or map it to a body part. Keeping it separate honors what Paul actually wrote.

---

## Decision 2: "Above all" (Ephesians 6:16) treatment on the site

**Question:** Should the site present one meaning of "above all" or acknowledge both?

**Decision:** Mention both meanings simply. Present the physical reality (a shield is held above and over the body, covering all the other armor) alongside the priority reading (faith is preeminent among the pieces). Let the visitor see both without declaring one definitive.

---

## Decision 3: Sword visual treatment on the diagram

**Question:** Should the sword look visually distinct from the five defensive pieces?

**Decision:** No. The sword uses the same visual style as all other armor pieces on the diagram.

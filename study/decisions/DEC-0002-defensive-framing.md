## Decision 1: Framing of the armor's purpose

**Question:** Is the armor primarily defensive (hold your ground) or does the sword make it more actively offensive?

**Decision:** Defensive framing is correct. The goal is to stand. Site copy and explanations should frame each piece in terms of holding ground, enduring, and withstanding — not conquering or advancing.

**Basis:** "Stand" or "withstand" appears three times in Ephesians 6:11–14. The Greek confirms it: five defensive pieces, one short-range sword (*machaira*), Paul omits the lance (the aggressive weapon) from the standard *panoplia*.

---

## Decision 2: How to present the 5:1 defensive asymmetry

**Question:** Should the site explicitly point out the asymmetry (five defensive, one offensive)?

**Decision:** No. Present each piece faithfully. Let the visitor notice the pattern on their own. Do not editorialize it in copy.
